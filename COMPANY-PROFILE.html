Electrical & Electronics
  • Industrial motor rewinding, PCB card repair, transformer rewinding, and complete marine electrical system servicing for both onboard and onshore applications.

Fabrication & Welding
  • Steel structure fabrication, hull repair, pipe works, buoys fabrication, and all types of welding by Bureau Veritas-approved certified welders.

NDT Inspection & Testing
  • Ultrasonic thickness gauging, ultrasonic flaw detection, magnetic particle inspection, dye penetrant testing, and hardness testing — all performed to classification society standards.

  • Industrial protective coatings, Belzona applications, sandblasting, grit blasting, and metal stitching services to protect and extend the life of marine and industrial equipment.
  • Our coating solutions meet international maritime standards, providing long-lasting protection against corrosion, erosion, and chemical exposure.
HVAC & Refrigeration

Engine Parts & Spares

Turbocharger Spares

We supply complete turbocharger units and individual replacement parts for all major marine turbocharger brands. Each unit undergoes thorough testing and quality verification before dispatch. Navigation Systems
